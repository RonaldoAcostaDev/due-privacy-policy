Privacy Policy

Due

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Compose R.A Studio developed Due as a credit notebook for small businesses and entrepreneurs. It lets the user record what their customers buy on credit and what they pay, and what the business buys on credit from its suppliers and what it pays them; set due dates, credit limits, and payment promises; see an agenda of collections and reports; send reminders, receipts, and account statements; export spreadsheets; and make backups.

All the information entered in the application is stored only on the user's device. The application does not have its own server (backend), does not require creating an account, does not ask for user registration, and does not use any authentication method, such as logging in with Google or any other provider. Its record-keeping features work without an internet connection.

To remain free of charge, the application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob and uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and record privacy choices about advertising where the law requires it. These services never receive the customers, suppliers, amounts, or any other business information recorded in the application. The application does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own.

Information privacy and security are a priority for Compose R.A Studio. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles, where it is kept, and what choices and rights the user has.

Information Stored on the Device

The information created by the user, including, but not limited to:

  • Customers and suppliers: name, phone number, address, notes, credit limit, color, and whether the account is archived
  • Movements: type (credit sale, payment received, credit purchase, or payment made), amount, concept, notes, date, due date, and payment method (cash, transfer, card, or other)
  • Payment promises: amount, date, notes, and whether the promise was kept
  • Balances, overdue amounts, the age of each balance, payer ratings ("pays on time", "sometimes late", "often late"), agenda items, and reports, all calculated on the device from the recorded movements
  • Business details: business name, business phone number, and an optional business logo
  • Settings: currency and currency format, language, theme, default due date, reminder and receipt message texts, daily summary time, "Hide amounts", automatic backup preferences, guided tips, and app lock options

is stored exclusively in the application's private storage on the user's device. Compose R.A Studio does not collect, store, process, or have access to this information.

The data remains under the exclusive control of the user, unless they decide to share an export, a receipt, a statement, or a backup using the features available in the application.

To suggest the currency when the application is set up, it reads the country code of the SIM card and of the mobile network, and the region configured on the phone. This does not use the device's location (GPS), does not require any permission, happens on the device, and is not sent anywhere.

Information About Customers and Suppliers

Due is used to keep records about other people, such as the user's customers and suppliers. This information is entered by the user, stays on the user's device, and is never sent to Compose R.A Studio or to advertisers. Due never contacts the user's customers or suppliers on its own: messages, receipts, and statements are sent only when the user chooses to send them, from the messaging application the user picks.

The user is responsible for the information they record about other people and for using it in accordance with the laws that apply to their business, for example by recording only what is needed to manage credit and collections and by keeping their device and backups protected.

Phone Contacts

When adding a customer or supplier, the user can choose "Pick from phone contacts". This opens Android's contact picker, and the application receives only the name and phone number of the one contact the user selects. Due does not request the contacts permission, cannot read the user's address book, and does not upload or store any other contact.

Business Logo

The business logo is optional and is added only when the user chooses an image with Android's photo picker, which gives the application access only to the image the user selects. The application keeps its own copy of the logo in its private storage, resized and saved again as a WebP image, so the copy does not carry the original file's metadata, such as the place where a photo was taken. The logo is shown in the application and on the receipts, statements, and reports the user creates, and it is deleted when the user removes it or deletes all the information.

App Lock, Biometrics, and Hiding Amounts

From Settings → Security and privacy → App lock, the user can protect the application with a PIN and, optionally, with the device's biometrics (fingerprint or face, depending on the device):

  • The PIN is never stored: the application keeps only a salted cryptographic hash of it (PBKDF2), on the device.
  • Biometric verification is performed by Android. The application only learns whether the verification succeeded; it never receives or stores fingerprints, face data, or any other biometric information.
  • The lock settings are kept separately and are never included in backup files or in Android's backup.
  • "Hide in recent apps" shows a blank card in the app switcher and blocks screenshots of the application.

For the user's privacy, a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered. In that case, the user can clear the application's data from the device settings and restore a backup.

"Hide amounts" masks the figures on the screen, for example when customers can see the phone at the counter. It changes only what is displayed; the recorded information is not modified.

What the Application Does Not Do

Due:

  • Does not require creating an account
  • Does not ask for the user's name, email, or phone number to use the application
  • Does not use authentication through Google, Facebook, or other services
  • Does not sync the recorded information with its own servers or any other server
  • Does not process payments, connect to bank accounts, or check anyone's credit score
  • Does not send messages, make calls, or contact the user's customers or suppliers on its own
  • Does not include analytics or crash reporting tools of its own
  • Does not access the device's location (GPS), address book, microphone, camera, call log, or SMS messages
  • Does not access the photo gallery or files beyond the items the user selects
  • Does not send, sell, or share the customers, suppliers, amounts, or other business information recorded by the user with anyone, including advertisers

Third-Party Services

The application uses the following third-party services. They may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

The application shows full-screen advertisements (interstitials) served by Google AdMob. They never appear when the application opens, during the welcome and setup screens, during the first 24 hours after the application is installed, or while the user is noting a sale or a payment. They appear only occasionally, after the user has completed several actions, such as noting a credit sale or a payment, adding a customer or supplier, sending a reminder, a receipt, or a statement, exporting a report or a spreadsheet, or saving a backup, with at least two minutes between two advertisements. They are shown at a natural pause, such as when the user closes the confirmation screen after noting a movement.

To serve and measure advertisements, limit how often they appear, prevent fraud, and, where the user allows it, personalize them, AdMob may collect:

  • The device's advertising ID
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived
  • Device and operating system information
  • Interactions with advertisements, such as impressions and clicks
  • Performance and diagnostic information of the advertising SDK, including crash logs

On devices that support them, AdMob may also use Android's advertising APIs of the Privacy Sandbox (such as Topics and Attribution), which the user can manage or turn off in the device settings.

The customers, suppliers, amounts, and other information recorded in the application are never shared with AdMob and are not used to choose advertisements. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google User Messaging Platform (Privacy Choices)

The application uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform provided by Google, to request and record privacy choices about advertising as required in each region. These choices are stored on the device and passed to Google's advertising services.

Google Play Billing

The application includes Google Play's billing library so that optional in-app purchases can be offered through Google Play. If a purchase is offered and the user makes it, it is processed entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered in Google Play and are never received by the application or by Compose R.A Studio. Google handles the purchase, its receipt, and any refund according to the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Sharing Initiated by the User

Some features open other applications. They are used only when the user requests them, and the user can review the content before sending it:

  • Reminders: "Remind" opens WhatsApp, the SMS application, or the Android share menu with a prepared message that includes the customer's name, balance, due date, and the business name. The message is sent only if the user sends it from that application.
  • Receipts and statements: the user can send a receipt image after a movement, or an account statement in PDF with the movements and balance of one customer or supplier, through WhatsApp or the Android share menu.
  • Calls: "Call" opens the phone's dialer with the contact's number; the call is made only if the user places it. Due does not request the phone permission.
  • Reports and exports (PDF, Excel, and CSV): from Reports, or from Settings → Data → Backup and export, the user can create a report in PDF or export their accounts and movements as an Excel file (.xlsx) or a CSV file, and save it in the location they choose or share it through the Android share menu.
  • Backup files: a backup can likewise be saved or shared, as described below.
  • Contact and sharing buttons: "Contact support" opens the user's email application with a prepared message that includes the application version, the Android version, and the device model; "Share the app" and "Rate the app" open the share menu or Google Play. Nothing is sent until the user confirms it.

Once information is shared, it is handled by the application or service the user chose, according to its own privacy policy. To share a file, the application creates a temporary copy of it in its private cache, which Android may clear at any time and which is deleted when the application's data is cleared or the application is uninstalled.

Backups

From Settings → Data → Backup and export, the user can create a backup file (.duebackup) with all the information of the application: customers, suppliers, movements, payment promises, business details and logo, currency settings, and message texts. The lock PIN and the other lock settings are never included. The file is created only when the user requests it and is saved in the location the user chooses or sent with the application the user picks, such as a cloud storage service or email. If that location is synchronized with a cloud storage service, that service's own privacy policy applies.

The user can also turn on automatic backups. The user chooses a folder and a frequency, and the application then writes a backup file into it periodically, when the battery and the storage are not low, keeping only the 10 most recent automatic files. The application can access only the folder the user chose, and automatic backups can be turned off at any time.

Backup files are not encrypted and contain the information about the user's customers and suppliers, so they should be kept in a safe place. Restoring a backup replaces all the information currently stored in the application.

If backup is enabled in the device's Android settings, Android may also include the records database, the settings, and the business logo of the application in the backup of the user's Google account or in a device-to-device transfer. The lock settings are never included and always stay on the original device. That backup is managed by Google under the user's account, and Compose R.A Studio has no access to it. It can be turned off from the device's backup settings.

Notifications

If the user allows notifications, the application can show a daily summary at the time the user chooses, with the number of collections that are overdue, due today, or promised for today, and their total amount. Depending on the device's notification settings, this summary may appear on the lock screen. The daily summary can be turned off in the application, and notifications can be disabled at any time from the device settings.

Data Security

The information is kept in the application's private storage, which Android isolates from other applications, and benefits from the storage encryption of the device where it is available. The optional app lock adds a PIN or biometric check to the application. To protect the information further, it is recommended to use a screen lock on the device and to keep backup files, statements, and exports in a safe place.

Data Retention and Deletion

The information stored on the device remains there until the user deletes it. The user can:

  • Edit or delete customers, suppliers, movements, and payment promises from within the application. Deleting a movement can be undone for a few seconds with "Undo"
  • Delete everything from Settings → Data → Backup and export → Delete everything, which removes all accounts, movements, settings, and the business logo from the device
  • Delete backup files, statements, and exports from the location where they were saved, and turn off automatic backups
  • Delete all the information by clearing the application's data from the device settings or by uninstalling the application

Backups, exports, receipts, statements, and other files saved or shared by the user remain wherever the user saved or sent them until the user deletes them. Because Compose R.A Studio does not operate servers or user accounts, it does not hold a copy of the user's information that it could access or delete.

Information collected by Google's services is retained according to Google's own policies and retention settings. For requests about that information, the user can contact Compose R.A Studio, which will help direct them to the right place.
